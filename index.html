    Tamil text & structure

    Thirukkural · திருக்குறள்

    The Tamil couplets, transliterations, and chapter structure are derived from tk120404/thirukkural, following the standard Parimelalagar-based recension. The source dataset is licensed under Apache 2.0; our generated data keeps the attribution in its file header.

    View the source dataset → Read the Apache 2.0 license

    Historical English source

    G. U. Pope · 1886

    English verse translations and prose explanations are attributed to G. U. Pope, with W. H. Drew, John Lazarus, and F. W. Ellis (1886). This historical work is in the public domain and is included through the upstream dataset.

    The plain-English meanings are newly written for Tamil Stoic from Pope’s prose; they are not Pope’s own wording.

    Project writing & editorial work

    Modern meanings and corrections

    The modern simple-English meanings are original to this project. Textual corrections are recorded with reasons and references; the review notes explain what has and has not been proofread.

    Reuse & transparency

    Open by design

    The project has no third-party JavaScript runtime dependencies or hosted font/script requests. Listen uses speech voices supplied by your browser or device; they are not bundled. Saved items remain on your device.
